# Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon — Key Facts

Organised summary of the official participant guide. Source: unorganised text
provided by the participant on October 28, 2026. Where this summary and the
official guide disagree, the official guide wins.

- **Our track: Indie Developer** (total $400,000: 1st $150K, 2nd $100K,
  3rd $75K, 4th $50K, 5th $25K)

  Community Vote $10K (announced Nov 16), Best Agentic Use-Case $5K,
  Best Creative Build $5K (Muse Image or Muse Wonder)

## Problem statements (must build towards one)

1. **Build for Your World** — Bring Meta's models into your domain. Solve a
   problem you know firsthand, free up time and mental space, and make
   something possible that wasn't before.
2. **Push the Frontier** — Use Meta's models to tackle challenges in software,
   robotics, manufacturing, energy, materials and beyond.

Required: the project must incorporate Meta's models — Muse Spark series,
Muse Wonder, Muse Image, Muse Voice Transcribe and SAM 3.1 via the Meta
Model API; open-weight Muse Glimmer and Muse Spark Open; Muse Code as
coding agent.

## Rules

- Repositories must be **public**.
- Team size: maximum 4; solo is allowed.
- **New work only:** the demo must only highlight features, code and
  functionality built during the hackathon. Judges must be able to clearly
  identify original contributions; failure means disqualification at the
  judges' discretion. Startups may build on existing products but must
  clearly identify what is new.
- Disqualification: legal/ethical/platform-policy violations, or using code,
  data or assets without rights.
- Anti-projects to avoid (generic or banned): AI medical advice (e.g. mental
  health advisor, skin blemish detector), basic RAG applications, simple
  image analyzers, simple "AI for Education" chatbots, AI job application
  screener, AI nutrition coach, personality analyzers, sports
  analyzers/coaches, anything political or involving war.

## Submission

- One project per team, submitted by an appointed Representative via the
  CV platform by **Nov 4, 9:00 PM ET**. Late submissions not considered.
- **3-minute demo video** required, highlighting only what was built during
  the hackathon. The video is a primary judging artifact, along with the
  code and live product.
- Double-check: public repository, accessible video link, all contributors
  listed.

## Judging

### Stage One (pass/fail)

Baseline viability: the project reasonably fits the theme and reasonably
used the Meta Model API and/or Meta's open-weight models.

### Stage Two (0–10 per category)

| Category | Weight | What it asks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Impact | 30% | Real-world potential; who benefits; could people actually use it; fit to a problem statement |
| Use of Meta AI Tooling | 25% | Creative use beyond a basic integration; surprising capabilities |
| Technical Depth & Execution | 20% | Sound, thoughtfully refined engineering |
| Demo | 25% | Working, impressive, holds up live; video genuinely cool to watch |

### Stage Three

Top 5 per track advance. Judges watch the video demos live and re-rank on
the same rubric.
